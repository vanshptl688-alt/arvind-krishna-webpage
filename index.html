About Arvind Krishna

A visionary leader transforming enterprise technology

Arvind Krishna is the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of IBM, one of the world's most influential technology corporations. Born in Andhra Pradesh, India, he graduated from the prestigious Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur before earning his PhD from the University of Illinois at Urbana‑Champaign. He has spent over three decades at IBM rising through the ranks to the company's highest position.

Krishna is widely credited with steering IBM's strategic pivot toward hybrid cloud and artificial intelligence — a transformation that repositioned the century-old company for the modern era of computing. He led IBM's landmark $34 billion acquisition of Red Hat in 2019, the largest software deal in history at that time, cementing IBM's open‑source and cloud capabilities.

Under his leadership, IBM has become a leading force in enterprise AI, quantum computing, and cybersecurity. Krishna is a member of the U.S. President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology (PCAST) and has been a vocal advocate for responsible AI, STEM education, and the importance of immigration reform to sustain innovation in America.

Major Contributions

Milestones that defined IBM's modern identity

☁️

Hybrid Cloud Strategy

Architected IBM's transformation into a hybrid cloud-first company, enabling enterprises to run workloads across on-premises, private, and public clouds with unified management — a framework now used by thousands of global organisations.

🔴

Red Hat Acquisition ($34 Billion)

Orchestrated the largest software acquisition in history, integrating Red Hat's open-source Linux and Kubernetes ecosystem into IBM's portfolio and cementing IBM's dominance in enterprise cloud infrastructure.

🤖

IBM Watson & Enterprise AI

Directed the evolution of Watson AI from a quiz-show novelty into a suite of production-grade AI services spanning natural language processing, machine learning, and automation for healthcare, banking, and supply-chain sectors.

⚛️

Quantum Computing Leadership

Championed IBM Quantum, making real quantum processors available over the cloud (IBM Quantum Experience) and driving research that placed IBM among the global leaders in quantum hardware and algorithm development.

🔐

Cybersecurity & Trustworthy AI

Expanded IBM Security's portfolio and championed the concept of AI ethics and explainability, launching the IBM AI Ethics Board and publishing principles for responsible AI adoption across industries.

🏭

Kyndryl Spin-off

Led the historic 2021 spin-off of IBM's managed infrastructure services division into Kyndryl, the world's largest IT infrastructure services provider, allowing IBM to sharpen its strategic focus on high-value hybrid cloud and AI.
